## Quality checks required before any line admission

No candidate has passed these checks in this block. The list is an audit gate, not a claim that a pipeline has certified data:

1. Record provider terms and permitted internal research use, upstream provenance, immutable source identity and acquisition budget. Do not redistribute restricted raw rows in a PR.
2. Resolve regular-season MLB games by exact official/provider IDs; certify doubleheaders and actual first pitch. No fuzzy player or event matching.
3. Preserve sportsbook/exchange distinction, cumulative period, home/away or named team, side, exact handicap/total and main/alternate designation. Never infer period from a generic spread/total or relabel an individual inning as F3/F5/F7.
4. Verify the original quote capture precedes actual start; a database insertion timestamp or `active` flag alone is insufficient. Never pass postgame records, outcomes or later corrections as pregame features.
5. Measure seasons, months, independent games, quotes per book, missingness and repeated snapshots separately. Verify both sides, valid odds and settlement rules; no invented lines or aggregate accuracy from metadata.
6. Keep any first sample quarantined and research-only. Dataset certification precedes model development; no retrospective recommendations or external opening.

## Next permitted action and real blocker

It is **not** a certified source: credentials/entitlement, original capture semantics, market mappings, and upstream provenance are unresolved.
