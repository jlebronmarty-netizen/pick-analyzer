# Pick Analyzer V1 Limitations

- MLB is the only production-core sport in V1.
- NFL and NHL are preview only.
- NBA, Soccer, BSN and UFC are data-only or partial.
- Tennis is unavailable.
- Official Picks are policy-gated and may be zero on a given day.
- All available Odds API data has not been downloaded.
- Historical odds coverage is not complete.
- Flat all-sport 5-minute polling is not certified.
- Adaptive 5-10 minute MLB refresh depends on credentials, provider quota, budget guards and scheduler health.
- Unsupported markets are not available recommendations.
- Automatic model training is disabled.
- Challenger model training and promotion require a future approved phase.
- Data Coverage compact route latency is variable and should be monitored post-release.
- Operations health may report DEGRADED when one or more operational dependencies need attention.
